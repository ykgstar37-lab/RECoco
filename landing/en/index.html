RECoco

A journal that prints your day as receipts and tickets

Books you read, films you watched, money you spent, trips you took, photo booth strips with friends — RECoco prints them like real receipts and tickets and keeps them in one long roll. No sign-up, no server: your records stay on your phone only.
Download on the App Store

FAQ

Will my records move to a new phone?

Records live only on your phone, so they do not move by themselves. Use Settings → 백업 파일 만들기 (Create backup file) to export a file, then 백업 파일 불러오기 (Load backup file) on the new phone.

What happens if I delete the app?

Your records and photos are deleted with it. Make a backup file in Settings first.

Can the developer see my records?

No. RECoco has no server, so what you write and the photos you attach are never sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I found a bug or have a feature idea

Send it from Settings → 버그 신고하기 (Report a bug) in the app, or email yge0307@gmail.com. A screenshot helps us fix it faster.
