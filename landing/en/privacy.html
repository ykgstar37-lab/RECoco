Privacy Policy

Effective: October 26, 2026 · Last updated: October 26, 2026

RECoco (the "app") has no sign-up and no server. What you write and the photos you attach are stored on your phone only — not even the developer can see them.

1. What stays on your phone

All of the following stays on the device and is never sent anywhere.

  • All your records (books, movies, spending, travel, photo booth, gifts, cafes & restaurants, shows & exhibitions, concerts, exercise, music, daily)
  • Photos attached to records (copied into the app's own folder, so a record keeps its photo even if you delete it from your album)
  • The categories, themes and outfits you have, and your settings

Deleting the app deletes this data too. To move it, export a single backup file from Settings → 백업 파일 만들기 (Create backup file) and keep it yourself. Where that file goes depends on what you choose in the share sheet.

2. What leaves your phone

WhenWhatWhere
Searching for a book, film or shopOnly the search words you typeKakao (books, places), TMDB (films)
Importing photos with a photo booth QR codeA request to open the photo page in the QR code (the app sends nothing else)That photo booth's photo page
Opening the app (update check)App version, device type (iOS/Android)Expo (app update server)
Sending a bug reportWhat you write, the contact you leave, screenshots you pick, app version, device model and OS version, number of recordsThe developer's email
Buying somethingPayment is handled by the App Store. The app only learns what was boughtApple

Search words are used only to fetch results and are not stored. Record content and photos are never included in a search. Bug reports are sent only when you send one, and a contact address is needed only if you want a reply.

3. What we do not do

  • No ads
  • No tracking or analytics tools
  • No location (shop search uses only the words you type)
  • We never sell or hand over any data to third parties

4. Permissions

PermissionUsed when
CameraScanning book, boarding pass and voucher barcodes, and photo booth QR codes
PhotosPicking photos to attach, or screenshots of payment alerts and bookings (only the photos you pick are read; text in a screenshot is read on the device and never sent)
Save to PhotosSaving a receipt image to your album

Each is asked only when you use that feature. If you decline, everything else keeps working.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Records stay on your phone until you delete them, and are deleted with the app.
  • Bug report emails are deleted once the problem is solved.

6. Children

RECoco does not ask for age and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
